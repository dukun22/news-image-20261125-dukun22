# The World Today

A make.fun entry for the [News Image 25 November 2026 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125): one poster summing up the whole day's world news, Wednesday 25 November 2026.

## The six stories

1. **Trump unveils new tariffs on 14 partners** — the USTR, after an 8-month Section 301 probe of 16 partners, announced new tariffs: China 20% on top of ~25% existing duties; the EU, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan capped at 15%; Malaysia 19%. Mexico and Norway are exempt, with probes ongoing. Markets fell: KOSPI -6%, Nikkei -3%.
2. **Pongkor: 9 dead, 17 still trapped** — an illegal gold mine in Pongkor, Bogor, flooded on Monday after heavy rain sent the Ciseureuh river into a pit about 100 m deep. 325 rescuers are at the site; 11 miners escaped.
3. **Huawei convicted in US trial** — a Brooklyn jury found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including trade-secret theft conspiracy, bank and wire fraud, Iran sanctions violations and money laundering. Sentencing is set for 14 September 2027.
4. **Ukraine strikes Russian air base, 5 planes damaged** — the Savasleyka air base in Nizhny Novgorod was hit, damaging 3 MiG-31 and 2 Su-34 aircraft.
5. **Putin vows retaliation for refinery strikes** — "Retaliation is inevitable." Russian crude runs are down about 45% since late October, and 15 refineries have been hit this month.
6. **Hormuz: 2 more tankers hit mines (CLAIM)** — the IRGC Navy claims two more tankers exploded and burned after hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz. Labelled CLAIM, UNVERIFIED: no vessel was named and UKMTO has not confirmed it.

All stories were verified against Reuters, AP, Xinhua and IRNA reporting.

This is a full-day revision: it replaces the morning-only version of the same poster, updating the tariff story from a planned announcement to the real one, the Pongkor toll to the AP evening figures, and swapping in the Huawei verdict and the Savasleyka strike for the morning's thinner stories.
